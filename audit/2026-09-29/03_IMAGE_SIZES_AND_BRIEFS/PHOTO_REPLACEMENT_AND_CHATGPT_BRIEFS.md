# Image replacement rules — exact dimensions without fabricated operating proof

The CSV separates live observed CSS display sizes from proposed default slots and exact master exports. Proposed desktop sizes assume a 1440px viewport; body images sit in the 1280px container. Mobile assumes a 390px viewport and 20px side padding (350px content). At 1920px, the body stays 1280px while full-bleed heroes widen; select the listed 3840px original where needed. Heights in the schedule are design targets and must be allowed to grow for wrapped text. Test actual source crops and verify no content clipping.

HD exports are native vector/raster design references included in 04_HD_ASSETS, with actual dimensions checked. They are not documentary mineral/port/company photographs. The assets are abstract and explicitly illustrative. Workflow/evidence text should be deployed as semantic HTML; the SVG/PNG is a visual reference or decorative illustration only.

For every documentary photograph: obtain original bytes, creator, capture date, rights permission, verified location/material, and any required privacy approval. Export from an original at least as large as the target crop. Do not upscale a 1024px image to 4K and claim detail was recovered. Keep crop subject clear at desktop/mobile/DPR2. Use no baked-in words or pseudo-certificates. Store licensing and original dimensions in the asset record.
