# Corridorway Audit Pack

Start with REPORT.md. It contains the executive findings and sections A–M of the requested audit and handoff, with explicit tested and untested coverage.

- defect-register.csv: 26 open prioritized findings with owners, effort, dependencies and measurable acceptance.
- image-manifest.csv: important observed HTML image occurrences and replacement guidance.
- claim-evidence-register.csv: initial claim verification queue; no supporting record is invented.
- verification-backlog.csv: tests needed before final certification/scoring.
- evidence/: point-in-time desktop screenshots and live browser DOM observations.

No production change, real inquiry submission, penetration test or numerical performance score was performed. Proposed tokens and copy require company approval. Browser-reported natural dimensions may be density-corrected. The URL inventory is not sitemap-certified exhaustive.
